ABOUT HAWA GOLD

Quality construction with a professional approach.

Hawa Gold Construction & Services is a Malaysian construction and services business based in Banting, Selangor. We focus on practical, quality-driven solutions for building, renovation, repair and external works.

From the first site discussion to completion, our aim is simple: clear communication, proper workmanship and a finished result that our customers can be proud of.
